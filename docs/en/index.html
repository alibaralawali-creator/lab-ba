FAQ

Is Lab BA really free?

Yes, all current features and updates are free.

Do I need internet?

Only to register and to update. Calculations and PDFs work offline, and your projects stay on your computer.

Mac or Linux?

Not yet: Lab BA runs on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

Are the results enough for a real project?

Lab BA is a study and preliminary design tool: results must be checked by a qualified engineer.
